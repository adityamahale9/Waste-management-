Materials Recycling Guide

Glass
  • Clean bottles and jars
  • Remove lids and caps
  • Separate by color if required
  • Avoid windows, mirrors, ceramics
Paper
  • Newspapers, magazines, catalogs
  • Office paper and mail
  • Paperboard and cardboard boxes
  • Keep dry and clean
Plastic
  • Check recycling numbers (1-7)
  • Rinse containers thoroughly
  • Flatten to save space
  • Avoid plastic bags (recycle separately)
Electronics
  • Computers, phones, TVs
  • Batteries and chargers
  • Small appliances
  • Often require special drop-off
Organic
  • Food scraps and yard waste
  • Coffee grounds and filters
  • Paper towels and napkins
  • Suitable for composting
Hazardous
  • Paint and solvents
  • Chemicals and pesticides
  • Motor oil and automotive fluids
  • Requires special disposal facilities
